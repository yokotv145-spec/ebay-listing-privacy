Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

About This Application

Yoko eBay Listing Tool is a private application intended to help its owner create and manage listings on their own eBay seller account. It is not offered to the public.

Information Accessed

With the account owner's authorization, the application may access eBay account information, listing details, inventory data, and authorization tokens needed to manage listings.

Use of Information

Information is used only to authenticate the account owner and create, update, or manage eBay listings.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the application, including eBay and authorized AI or hosting services, where applicable.

Data Security and Retention

Reasonable measures are taken to protect credentials and access tokens. Information is retained only as necessary for operating the application and is deleted when no longer required, subject to applicable obligations.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the application owner through the associated GitHub account: yokotv145-spec.
